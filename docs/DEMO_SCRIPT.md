# Signal Stack – Demo Recording Script & Walkthrough Guide

Use this script when recording the project presentation video or conducting a live stakeholder demonstration.

---

## Act 1: Introduction & Problem Context (0:00 - 1:00)
1. **Hook:** "Welcome to Signal Stack, the autonomous competitive and market intelligence engine built for enterprise leadership."
2. **Context:** "In fast-evolving markets like international higher education—exemplified by MeetUniversity—regulatory visa caps in Canada and Australia, aggressive competitor commission wars, and internal operational delays can blindside a leadership team."
3. **Value Proposition:** "Signal Stack continuously monitors permitted public sources, runs an explainable 6-factor ranking algorithm, ingests internal operations, and automatically delivers a 10-section Monday Morning Intelligence Brief with tracked action items."

---

## Act 2: MeetUniversity Overview Dashboard (1:00 - 2:30)
1. **Navigate to:** `Overview Dashboard`
2. **Demonstrate:**
   - Active Organization header showing MeetUniversity (`meetuniversity.com`).
   - High-level metric KPI cards: Total Verified Signals, 20 Monitored Sources, 100% Traceability Rate, 8 Tracked Competitors.
   - The 4 Pillar breakdown: Customer, Competitor, Market, and Internal.
   - Click the **"Score: 92.5/100"** badge on a top signal to reveal the **Explainable Ranking Score Modal**:
     - Point out the 6 mathematical components: Strategic Relevance (25%), Business Impact (25%), Action Urgency (20%), Credibility (15%), Recency (10%), and Novelty (5%).
   - Click **"Run Crawl Sweep"** to show live crawler execution across sources.

---

## Act 3: 10-Question Adaptive Diagnostic Interview (2:30 - 4:00)
1. **Navigate to:** `AI Diagnostic Interview`
2. **Demonstrate:**
   - 10 structured diagnostic questions spanning the 4 pillars.
   - Progress bar showing completion rate.
   - Click into Question 1 (Student Drop-Offs): Show the Ground-Truth Answer.
   - Point out the **Adaptive Follow-Up Question** and the **Signal Synthesis Takeaway** automatically generated based on the executive's specific response.
   - Show how editing an answer updates the strategic takeaway in real time.

---

## Act 4: The 4 Signal Stack Pillars & Source Manager (4:00 - 5:30)
1. **Navigate to:** `Signal Stack`
   - Switch between **Customer**, **Competitor**, **Market**, and **Internal** tabs.
   - Show direct quotes and evidence links for real developments (e.g., IRCC Canada 10% study permit reduction, UK Graduate Route retention, LeverageEdu Fly Finance loan bundles).
2. **Navigate to:** `Source Manager`
   - Highlight the **20 verified public sources for MeetUniversity** (The PIE News, ICEF Monitor, UK Home Office, ApplyBoard Newsroom, Reddit communities, Trustpilot).
   - Show documented **Relevance Explanations** for every single source.
   - Click **"Run Monthly Health Audit"** to demonstrate live HTTP status code verification and latency checking.

---

## Act 5: Internal Intelligence & Telemetry Ingestion (5:30 - 6:45)
1. **Navigate to:** `Internal Intelligence`
2. **Demonstrate:**
   - Click **"Load Sample Data"** to load the Q3 Admissions Intake Drop-Off Log.
   - Click **"Ingest & Extract Signals"**.
   - Show how the AI extracts 3 actionable internal signals, automatically calculates their 6-factor ranking score, and routes them directly into the Signal Stack!

---

## Act 6: Monday Morning Intelligence Brief (6:45 - 8:15)
1. **Navigate to:** `Monday Brief`
2. **Demonstrate:**
   - Show all **10 required sections** in executive document format:
     1. Executive Summary
     2. Customer Signals
     3. Competitor Intelligence
     4. Market Intelligence
     5. Internal Business Intelligence
     6. Opportunities Identified
     7. Risks and Threats
     8. Recommended Actions for This Week
     9. What We Should Watch
     10. Source Appendix & Citations
   - Check off an action item in the **Action Tracker** sidebar to show real-time persistence.
   - Click **"Download PDF"** to demonstrate the generated ReportLab executive PDF document!

---

## Act 7: Multi-Company Demonstrations & Conclusion (8:15 - 9:30)
1. **Switch Active Company:** In the top navigation dropdown, switch to **Stripe** or **Datadog**.
2. **Demonstrate:**
   - Show how the entire application dynamically recalibrates: new industry (FinTech / Observability), distinct competitors (Adyen, PayPal / Dynatrace, New Relic), new sources, and tailored Signal Stacks.
   - Show how changing the business context in **Company Profile** dynamically shifts strategic prioritization.
3. **Wrap-up:** "Signal Stack delivers complete, verifiable, explainable competitive intelligence with zero manual hallucination."
